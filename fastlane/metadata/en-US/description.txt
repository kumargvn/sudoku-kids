Sudoku Kids turns Sudoku into a friendly game that teaches children how to think step by step. It starts with tiny 4×4 picture puzzles and grows all the way to classic 9×9 Sudoku.

HINTS THAT TEACH
Stuck? A hint doesn't just reveal the answer. It highlights the row, column or box and explains why only one number fits, so kids learn the trick and can use it again. Hints can also be read out loud for children who are still learning to read.

PUZZLES FOR EVERY AGE
• Tiny 4×4, Junior 6×6 and Classic 9×9 boards
• Four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert
• Play with numbers, animals, fruit, sea life or colours
• Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and Easy and Medium can always be solved with the tricks the hints teach

LOTS TO DO
• Adventure: travel through 5 worlds and 30 levels, from Forest Friends to Dino Valley
• Daily Challenge: a new puzzle every day and a day streak to keep
• Learn: four short lessons with real examples and a quick quiz
• 17 trophies and stars to collect
• Notes, Auto notes, undo and an optional 3-mistake challenge

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• Up to 6 player profiles, each with their own progress
• A Grown-ups area behind a parental gate shows each child's progress and play time
• An optional break reminder
• Sounds and vibration can be turned off

SAFE AND FREE
Sudoku Kids is completely free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. Nothing is collected or shared, and all progress stays on the device.
