Sudoku Kids: Learn & Play

A free Sudoku app for iPhone and iPad that teaches children logic, one square at a time.

  • Tiny 4×4, junior 6×6 and classic 9×9 puzzles, from Easy to Expert
  • Pictures or numbers: animals, fruit, sea life or colours
  • Hints that explain why, and can be read out loud
  • An adventure map, a daily challenge, lessons and trophies
  • Profiles for each child and a grown-ups area with a break reminder
  • No ads, no purchases, no data collected
